Supplementary materials and data of the systematic review by Mohamed-Amine Lahkim-Bennani, Abdelhamid Rossi and Brunella Bonaccorso, submitted to *Water* (MDPI).

## Methods in brief

Scopus and the Web of Science Core Collection were searched in September and October 2026 for field studies published online between 1 January 2010 and 30 April 2026. Records were screened on titles and abstracts, and 133 studies were included. Each study was coded for ten categories of methods and appraised on four criteria. Full details are given in the article.

## Note on database records

The records exported from Scopus and Web of Science are not redistributed here, because their licences do not allow it. They can be reproduced by running the search strings in `search/`.
